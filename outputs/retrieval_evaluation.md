1.2 Refrigerated Storage
Refrigerated storage (2C to 8C) is acceptable for short-term use immediately prior to administration. Product must never be refrozen once thawed. Vials should be kept upright and protected from light at all times, as mRNA formulations are photosensitive and repeated freeze-thaw cycles degrade the lipid nanoparticle structure.
1.3 Transport Conditions
During transport, validated cold-chain shipping containers with continuous temperature logging are required. Any excursion above -15C for formulations requiring ultra-low storage must be documented and reviewed before the affected batch is approved for patient use. Deviations lasting longer than 1 hour require the batch to be quarantined pending quality review.
1.4 Visual Inspection Prior to Use
Prior to administration, vials must be visually inspected for particulate matter or discoloration. Product exhibiting visible particulates, cloudiness inconsistent with the expected formulation appearance, or any container damage must not be administered and should be reported through the standard product-quality complaint process.

Ages 65 and older: standard adult dosing range applies; however, patients in this age band with two or more relevant pre-existing conditions should be flagged for secondary clinician review regardless of dose, due to elevated reactogenicity risk observed in this population.

Patients presenting with an inflammatory biomarker level meaningfully above the normal reference range at the time of screening should have dosing deferred until the biomarker returns to a normal range, or should receive the lowest clinically appropriate dose tier with enhanced post-administration monitoring, at the administering clinician's discretion.

3.1 Absolute Contraindications
Known severe allergic reaction (e.g., anaphylaxis) to a prior dose of the same product or to any component of the formulation, including the lipid nanoparticle excipients.
Active, unresolved severe adverse reaction from a previous dose that has not been cleared by an allergist or treating physician.
3.2 Precautions Requiring Secondary Review
Three or more relevant pre-existing conditions at time of screening.
Prior history of a moderate reaction to an mRNA-based product, even if the reaction has since resolved.
Candidate dose exceeding 100 micrograms combined with an elevated biomarker level.
Age under 5 years combined with any pre-existing condition.
3.3 Standard Warnings
Patients should be monitored for at least 15 minutes following administration, or 30 minutes for patients with a documented history of any allergic reaction. Localized injection-site reactions (soreness, redness, mild swelling) are common and do not by themselves require secondary review. Systemic reactions such as fever, chills, or fatigue lasting more than 48 hours should be documented and reported.
Section 4: Adverse Event Documentation
4.1 Reporting Thresholds
Any adverse event classified as moderate or severe must be documented within 24 hours of occurrence, including onset time, duration, severity, and any treatment administered. Mild, self-limiting reactions consistent with expected reactogenicity do not require individual reporting but should be captured in aggregate monitoring where available.
4.2 Escalation Criteria
An adverse event should be escalated to clinical safety review if it involves hospitalization, is life-threatening, results in persistent disability, or is otherwise considered medically significant by the administering clinician.
